How to Switch Between Solid and Wireframe in Blender

Switching between solid and wireframe shading modes in Blender is an essential skill for modeling, editing geometry, and selecting hidden vertices. This guide covers the fastest ways to toggle between these display modes using keyboard shortcuts, viewport header buttons, and overlay settings in modern versions of Blender.

Method 1: The Pie Menu Shortcut (Fastest)

The quickest way to switch shading modes is using the Z hotkey.

  1. Hover your cursor over the 3D Viewport.
  2. Press Z on your keyboard to open the Shading Pie Menu.
  3. Move your mouse toward Wireframe (left) or Solid (right) and click, or press the corresponding number key.

Method 2: Viewport Shading Buttons

You can manually switch modes using the header interface at the top-right corner of the 3D Viewport.

  1. Look at the top-right corner of the 3D Viewport window.
  2. Click the Wireframe icon (a hollow sphere) to enter wireframe mode.
  3. Click the Solid icon (a solid sphere) to return to solid mode.

Method 3: Quick Toggle Shortcut

To instantly switch back and forth between your current shading mode and Wireframe mode without opening a menu:

  • Press Shift + Z on your keyboard.

This shortcut toggles your view directly between Wireframe and your previously active shading mode (such as Solid or Material Preview).

How to Display Wireframe Lines Over Solid Mode

If you want to view the wireframe layout on top of solid geometry without making the object fully transparent:

  1. Click the Viewport Overlays dropdown menu (the icon with two overlapping circles in the top-right header).
  2. Check the box labeled Wireframe.
  3. Adjust the Geometry Wireframe Slider to control the opacity of the wireframe lines.
