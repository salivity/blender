How to Enable Axis Display for Bones in Blender

Displaying bone axes in Blender is essential for rigging, as it allows 3D artists to clearly see the local orientation (X, Y, and Z axes) of every bone within an armature. This guide provides a quick, step-by-step walkthrough on how to enable the bone axis display setting in Blender's viewport properties so you can accurately adjust bone roll, set up constraints, and streamline your animation workflow.

Steps to Display Bone Axes in Blender

To make the local axes visible on your armature's bones, follow these steps:

  1. Select the Armature: Click on your armature in the 3D Viewport in Object Mode, Edit Mode, or Pose Mode.
  2. Open Data Properties: Go to the Properties panel (typically on the right side of the interface) and click on the Object Data Properties tab, represented by a green stick-figure icon.
  3. Expand Viewport Display: Scroll down to locate and click on the Viewport Display section to open its settings.
  4. Enable Axes: Check the box labeled Axes under the "Show" options.

Once checked, the local coordinate axes will immediately appear on all bones in the selected armature, displayed as Red (X), Green (Y), and Blue (Z) lines.

Why Viewing Bone Axes is Important

  • Adjusting Bone Roll: Blender automatically aligns the Y-axis along the length of the bone. Displaying axes allows you to adjust the bone roll (shortcut Ctrl + R in Edit Mode) to ensure left and right sides are symmetrical.
  • Setting Up Constraints: Constraints like Copy Rotation, Transformation, or Limit Rotation rely on local bone orientation. Seeing the axes helps you choose the correct target and owner axes.
  • Correcting Animation Axes: Viewing the axes ensures that rotational channels (pitch, yaw, and roll) behave predictably during keyframing.
