How to Toggle Perspective and Orthographic View in Blender

Switching between perspective and orthographic views in Blender is an essential skill for 3D modeling, allowing creators to view objects with realistic depth or precise, distortion-free alignment. This guide covers the quickest keyboard shortcuts, viewport gizmos, and menu options to seamlessly toggle your camera mode in Blender, even if you are working on a laptop without a dedicated number pad.

Method 1: The Numpad 5 Shortcut

The fastest way to switch between Perspective and Orthographic view is using the Numpad:

  1. Hover your mouse over the 3D Viewport.
  2. Press Numpad 5 on your keyboard.

Pressing Numpad 5 acts as a toggle. Repeating the keystroke switches the view back to the previous mode. You can check the top-left corner of the 3D Viewport to see your current mode (it will display either "User Perspective" or "User Orthographic").

Note: If your laptop lacks a number pad, go to Edit > Preferences > Input and check Emulate Numpad. This allows you to use the standard top-row number keys.

Method 2: The View Menu

If you prefer using mouse navigation instead of hotkeys, use the top menu bar in the 3D Viewport:

  1. Click on View in the top-left corner of the 3D Viewport.
  2. Select Perspective/Orthographic from the dropdown menu.

Method 3: Viewport Navigation Gizmo

You can also use the built-in navigation widget located in the upper-right corner of the 3D Viewport:

  1. Locate the Navigation Gizmo (the colored X, Y, Z axis sphere).
  2. Click the Grid Icon located directly below the zoom and pan icons.
  3. Clicking this icon instantly toggles the view between Perspective and Orthographic mode.

Method 4: Accent Grave / Tilde Pie Menu

For a quick menu-driven hotkey approach:

  1. Press the ` (Accent Grave / Tilde) key on your keyboard to open the View Pie Menu.
  2. Select Toggle Perspective/Orthographic from the menu options.

Additionally, using standard view alignment keys (such as Numpad 1 for Front, Numpad 3 for Side, or Numpad 7 for Top view) automatically switches your viewport into Orthographic mode to ensure accurate alignment.
