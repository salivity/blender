How to Snap 3D Cursor to World Origin in Blender

Resetting or snapping the 3D cursor to the world origin (0, 0, 0) in Blender is a fundamental step for precise modeling, object alignment, and managing pivot points. This guide covers the quickest keyboard shortcuts, menu options, and manual sidebar settings to instantly return your 3D cursor to the center of your workspace.

Method 1: The Quickest Keyboard Shortcut (Shift + S)

  1. Press Shift + S anywhere in the 3D Viewport to open the Snap Menu.
  2. Select Cursor to World Origin (or press 1 on your keyboard while the menu is open).

This instantly snaps the 3D cursor back to coordinates (0, 0, 0) without altering your current view or zoom level.

Method 2: Reset View and Cursor (Shift + C)

  1. Press Shift + C on your keyboard.

This shortcut performs two actions simultaneously: it moves the 3D cursor directly to the world origin and frames all objects in your scene within the view. This is useful if you have lost sight of both your cursor and your objects.

Method 3: Manual Input via the Sidebar (N-Panel)

If you prefer precise numerical control or want to verify the coordinates:

  1. Press N to open the Sidebar in the 3D Viewport.
  2. Click on the View tab.
  3. Expand the 3D Cursor panel.
  4. Set the Location values for X, Y, and Z to 0.
