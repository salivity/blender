What Is the Boundary Brush Used for in Blender?

The Boundary Brush in Blender is a dedicated sculpting tool designed specifically to deform and control the open edges and borders of a 3D mesh. Unlike traditional sculpting brushes that affect surrounding geometry equally, the Boundary Brush automatically detects open mesh perimeters, allowing artists to bend, stretch, expand, or smooth surface borders cleanly without distorting the interior topology.

Core Functionality of the Boundary Brush

When sculpting objects with open geometry—such as clothing, armor pieces, or hollowed-out shapes—standard brushes often cause undesirable pinching or uneven stretching along the edges. The Boundary Brush solves this by calculating the distance along the mesh's open boundary loops and applying targeted transformations.

When you place the brush near an open edge, it locks onto the boundary contour, letting you adjust the shape of the border relative to the rest of the object.

Deformation Modes

The Boundary Brush includes several deformation modes that dictate how the mesh edge reacts to your stroke:

  • Bend: Rotates and bends the edge loop around its local axis, perfect for curling fabric hems or rolling armor plating edges.
  • Expand: Extends the boundary outward away from the center of the surface, useful for widening openings.
  • Magnet: Pulls the boundary directly toward the brush cursor position.
  • Grab: Translates the targeted edge section in the direction of your brush stroke.
  • Twist: Rotates the boundary around the center of the brush stroke.
  • Smooth: Relaxes and flattens jagged or uneven boundary lines without shrinking the surrounding mesh excessively.

Primary Use Cases

  • Cloth and Garment Sculpting: Creating realistic folds, flared hems, rolled cuffs, and loose collar edges on 3D character clothing.
  • Hard-Surface and Armor Design: Shaping the rims of helmets, shoulder pads, or cutout details on mechanical parts.
  • Organic Openings: Refining interior borders like open mouths, eye sockets, or hollowed-out organic forms.

Why Use the Boundary Brush Over Standard Sculpting Tools?

Standard brushes move vertices purely based on a 3D spherical radius, which frequently collapses thin surfaces or twists open border loops. The Boundary Brush evaluates the actual mesh topology along the open boundary. This topology-aware behavior ensures that edge loops retain clean spacing and natural curvature, drastically reducing the time spent manually cleaning up geometry in Sculpt Mode.
