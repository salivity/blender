How to Set Up Custom Matcaps in Blender

Setting up custom Matcaps (Material Captures) in Blender allows you to quickly inspect surface topology, identify shading artifacts, and enhance your sculpting workflow. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to import, install, and activate custom Matcap textures directly through Blender’s preferences for use in the 3D Viewport.

Step 1: Prepare Your Matcap Image

Before importing, ensure your Matcap file is formatted correctly:

  • Format: PNG, TIFF, or OpenEXR (PNG is most common).
  • Shape: A spherical rendering of a material centered on a square canvas.
  • Resolution: 512×512 or 1024×1024 pixels works best.

Step 2: Open Blender Preferences

  1. Launch Blender.
  2. In the top application menu, click Edit.
  3. Select Preferences from the dropdown menu (or press Ctrl + , on Windows/Linux, Cmd + , on macOS).

Step 3: Install the Custom Matcap

  1. In the left-hand sidebar of the Preferences window, select the Lights tab.
  2. Expand the MatCaps section at the top of the panel.
  3. Click the Install... button located at the top-right of the MatCaps list.
  4. Use the file browser to locate and select your Matcap image file.
  5. Click Install MatCap File.

The new Matcap will now appear in your list of available viewport lighting presets and is saved globally across all future Blender projects.

Step 4: Apply the Custom Matcap in the 3D Viewport

  1. In the 3D Viewport, switch the shading mode to Solid (press Z and select Solid, or click the Solid circle in the top-right corner of the viewport).
  2. Click the small dropdown arrow next to the viewport shading spheres to open the Viewport Shading menu.
  3. Under the Lighting section, select the MatCap option.
  4. Click on the round Matcap preview ball to open the selection palette.
  5. Scroll to find and click on your newly installed custom Matcap.

Removing Custom Matcaps

To remove a custom Matcap later, return to Edit > Preferences > Lights > MatCaps, hover your cursor over the custom Matcap you wish to delete, and click the X icon that appears next to it.
