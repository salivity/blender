How to Select Random Faces in Blender

Selecting random faces in Blender is a straightforward process using the "Select Random" tool in Edit Mode. By switching to Face Select mode, opening the Select menu, and choosing "Select Random," you can instantly highlight an arbitrary percentage of geometry across your 3D mesh. This feature includes customizable settings to adjust the selection ratio and seed, making it ideal for creating greebles, applying varied materials, or building organic, non-uniform surface details.

Step-by-Step Guide to Selecting Random Faces

  1. Select Your Object: Click on the 3D mesh you want to edit in Object Mode.
  2. Enter Edit Mode: Press Tab on your keyboard, or select Edit Mode from the drop-down menu in the top-left corner of the Viewport.
  3. Switch to Face Select Mode: Press 3 on your top number row (not the Numpad), or click the Face Select icon (a solid square) in the top-left header.
  4. Open the Select Menu: Click Select in the top Viewport menu bar.
  5. Choose Select Random: Click Select Random from the drop-down list.

Blender will immediately select a random pattern of faces across the entire mesh.

Adjusting the Selection Parameters

Once you execute the command, an Adjust Last Operation panel appears in the bottom-left corner of the 3D Viewport (labeled "Select Random"). Click to expand it and fine-tune your selection:

  • Ratio: Controls the percentage of faces selected. A value of 0.50 selects roughly 50% of the total faces, while 0.10 selects 10%.
  • Random Seed: Changes the mathematical seed used for the randomization algorithm. Cycling through different seed numbers generates unique selection patterns while keeping the chosen ratio intact.
  • Action: Choose whether the tool Selects or Deselects faces randomly based on your current selection state.

Selecting Random Faces from an Existing Selection

If you only want to select random faces within a specific area of your model rather than the entire mesh:

  1. Manually select the target group of faces.
  2. Go to Select > Select Random.
  3. In the Adjust Last Operation panel (bottom-left), change the Action setting if needed, or simply adjust the Ratio to reduce the active selection to a random subset of those specific faces.
